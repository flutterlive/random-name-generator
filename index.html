How to pick a name that lasts

A good name does half your marketing for you. Four checks before you commit:

1

Say it out loud

If people cannot spell it after hearing it once — on a podcast, in a shop, over the phone — it will cost you customers. Short, clear names win.

2

Check the handle and domain match

Your Instagram, TikTok, YouTube handle and your .com (or .co.uk in the UK) should ideally be the same word. Search each platform before printing anything.

3

Check trademarks and company registers

In the USA, search the USPTO trademark database and your state registry. In the UK, check Companies House and the UK IPO. A two-minute check beats a forced rebrand.

4

Leave room to grow

"Denver Dog Grooming" is clear today but limiting if you add cats or a second city. Pick a name that still fits where the business is going, not only where it starts.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using the names you generate.

Is this name generator really free?

Yes. Generate as many business names, usernames and random names as you like — there is no sign-up, no credits and no paywall. Everything runs in your browser.

Can I use a generated name for my real business?

Yes, the ideas are yours to use. Just run the availability checks first: trademark registers (USPTO in the USA, UK IPO in the UK), company registration (Companies House in the UK, your state registry in the USA), the domain name, and the social media handles you need.

Does the tool check if a username or domain is available?

No. Availability changes every minute and differs per platform, so no generator can promise a handle or domain is free. Treat every result as a strong idea, then verify it on the platform itself before you commit.

What is the difference between the USA and UK settings?

The region switch changes the spelling flavour of business names (for example "Color" in the USA vs "Colour" in the UK, and shop suffixes like "Emporium" in the UK) and switches the random person name pools to names commonly used in that country.

How do I get more creative or unusual names?

Select the Creative or Fun styles, leave the keyword blank, and use Generate More a few times — the generator combines word parts in thousands of combinations, so each batch is different. Short & Punchy gives you compact, brandable one-word style names.

Can I generate names for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube channels?

Yes — use the Username tab, pick your platform, and add a topic keyword (like "fitness", "baking" or your gaming tag). YouTube results are formatted as channel names, while Instagram and TikTok results use handle-friendly formats with dots, underscores and prefixes.
